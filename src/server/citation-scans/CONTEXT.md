# Citation scans

Linking a MyHeritage church-book **Citation** to the Astia scan of the page it cites. Two archives meet here: MyHeritage shows FamilySearch's **Films**; Astia (the National Archives) holds **Volumes**. Neither numbers its images like the other.

## Language

**Citation**:
A MyHeritage source reference to a church-book record, naming its collection, parish, **Book**, years, page and **Record number**.

**Book**:
The kind of church book cited — rippikirja (communion book), lastenkirja (children's book), baptisms or marriages — with the years it covers. What a Citation names; a Book may span several **Volumes** or share one.

**Volume**:
One book in Astia, with its own Astia id and label (e.g. "I Aa:5 Pää- ja rippikirjat (1790-1796)"). The unit a link points into.
_Avoid_: film (that's the MyHeritage side), archive (the parish's fonds that holds the Volumes).

**Part**:
One of several Volumes a Book is split into, usually by village (I, II …). Told apart by the villages in their labels or found in them, or by where each one's page numbering runs.

**Image**:
One scan in a Volume, numbered from 1 in Astia's order. What a link opens.
_Avoid_: page (written on the scan), frame (MyHeritage's numbering).

**Page number**:
The number written in the book. Rippikirjat usually number spreads (one number per Image), lastenkirjat pages (two per Image). A Volume may start its numbering over partway through.

**Anchor**:
A Page number read off one Image of a Volume by hand. Images for other pages are counted from the nearest Anchors. Where MyHeritage's indexer misread a page number, the Anchor gives the number as cited and notes what's written.

**Film**:
FamilySearch's microfilm of the books, as MyHeritage shows it. One Film can hold several Volumes, and its order needn't follow Astia's.

**Frame**:
One image on a Film, numbered as MyHeritage numbers it. The Frame-to-Image offset varies even within one Film.

**Record number**:
MyHeritage's id for a record in the collection. Each Film's records form one unbroken block in Frame order, and blocks never overlap — so one record known to be on a Film places every record numbered around it there too.

**Record range**:
A Volume's span of Record numbers. Overrides choosing the Volume by years.

**Seen record**:
A record whose MyHeritage Frame was matched by eye to an Image, so its Image is known exactly.

**Same scan** / **Different photograph**:
Whether a Seen record's Frame is the very scan Astia holds (same framing, shadows, blots) or a separate filming of the same page, matched by its content — page number, or village, house and household where the book has no page numbers.
_Avoid_: "same page" alone — it doesn't say which.

**Retake**:
A second photograph of the same page within one Volume, on the next Image.

**Doubled volume**:
A Volume whose Images hold the book twice, as if filmed twice; the copies drift apart in Image numbers. Anchors point into the copy MyHeritage shows.

**Placement**:
A record put on an Image by hand in the app, for a Citation with no page or in a Volume its Book doesn't match.

**Scan method**:
How sure a link is, best first:

- **Placed** — a Seen record or a Placement: the exact Image.
- **Anchored** — counted from Anchors.
- **Estimated** — guessed from the Book's usual numbering.
- **Volume** — the Volume is known but not the Image; the link opens the Volume.

A Citation whose Volume can't be told apart gets one link per candidate: it is **Split**.

## Flagged ambiguities

- **"Volume"** is Astia's book; MyHeritage's equivalent is a **Film**, which may hold several Volumes. **"Image"** is Astia's numbering, **"Frame"** MyHeritage's, **"Page number"** what's written in the book.
- **"Placed"** covers both a **Seen record** and a **Placement**: either way the exact Image is known.
