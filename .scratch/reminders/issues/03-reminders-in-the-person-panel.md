# The person panel's Reminders

Spec: `.scratch/reminders/spec.md`. Glossary: **Reminder**, **Citation** (`src/server/citation-scans/CONTEXT.md`).

- **A Reminders section** in the person panel (`src/client/components/person-panel/`) listing the person's own open Reminders, shown only when there are any, or done ones with _Show done_ on.
- **A Citation's Reminders** shown beside that Citation in the sources, with a small marker on its row while it's folded.
- **Each Reminder's right-click menu**: _Done_ or _Reopen_, _Edit…_ (the Scan viewer's dialog, its _On_ fixed), _Delete_.
- **_Show done_**: a toggle in the section that shows done Reminders, dimmed, for this person and their Citations.
- Nothing is written here: a new Reminder starts in the Scan viewer.
