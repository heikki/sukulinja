# Cite an Image found by browsing the church books

Glossary: **Citation**, **Church-book reference**, **Scan viewer**, **Mark**, **Box**.

The Church books view opens a Volume in the Scan viewer with no source. There, the menu offers only a Reminder: "Create entry for…" needs a source, so a Citation can't yet be made from an Image found by browsing, and a tree with no references has no way to get its first one from the scans.

## The idea

From a browsed Image, create a Citation of it for someone: pick the person, draw the entry's Box, and save a Citation the app made, whose Link is Marked at the entry (as "Create entry for…" does today from a source).

## Open questions

- What the new Citation's reference says when nothing was cited: the parish, Book and years from the Volume's label, the Image number, and the page if it's written on the Image.
- Which fact of the person it cites (birth, marriage, a household in a rippikirja), and whether that's asked or left for later.
- Whether it shares the "Create entry for…" flow, which now starts from a source's Volume.
