# Color a Link by its Certainty

Glossary: **Link**, **Certainty**, **Mark**, **Match**, **Scan viewer**.

Idea: give each Certainty its own color — marked, match, estimated — so you can tell at a glance how far to trust a Link.

## Open questions

- **Where.** Boxes on the Scan viewer's Image, the entries grouped by Certainty beside the scan (`scan-viewer/citations.ts` already groups them: marked entry, marked image, match, estimated), the person panel's Links, or all of them.
- **Marked to an entry vs. only an Image.** The same color for both, or two shades.
- **Stale pins.** Whether a stale Mark or Match keeps its color, dimmed, or has its own.
- **Dark mode and contrast.** Three colors that read on both the scan and the panel, and don't lean on color alone (the label stays).
