# The _All books_ mode of the Volumes tab

A toggle at the top of the tab: _This tree_ | _All books_. _All books_ lists every Volume with anything on this computer, shared by every dataset: Images in the Scan cache, a download, Page pins. A table: Volume, Parish, Trees (how many datasets list it), Pages (not read / read / checked, ticket 04), State (on disk, downloading, part, offloaded later), Size. The Scan cache's fill against its cap above it, and filters (on disk, downloading, page numbers).

Actions: download / stop, remove from disk, and later offload (`.scratch/church-books/issues/03-archive-books-keep-cited-pages.md`). Settings' Scan cache section may move here.
