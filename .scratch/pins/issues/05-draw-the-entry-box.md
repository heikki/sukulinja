# Draw where the entry is when marking an Image

Glossary: **Box**, **Mark**, **Precision**.

A Mark without a Box makes a Link Marked Image: the person said the Image, not where on it. Drawing the entry's Box on the scan would make it Marked, at the entry, like a confirmed Vision match.

## Open questions (decide before agent work)

- How you draw: drag a rectangle on the scan, click an entry the model already found, or both.
- Whether drawing is part of every Mark, or an optional second step on a Marked Image Link.
- Whether the people citing the same record share the drawn Box, as they share a vision one.
- Editing or clearing a drawn Box, and whether a drawn Box replaces a vision one on the same Image (hand beats vision says yes).
