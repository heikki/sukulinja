# Mark only the Volume a reference is in

Glossary: **Mark**, **Reference pin**, **Precision**.

Some references are fine with a Volume Link, such as baptism index references, where a Volume is enough. But the Volume chosen by the Book's years or by a Record range can be the wrong one. In the Scan viewer you can already switch Volumes. A Volume-only Mark saves that choice: a Reference pin with the Volume and no Image, which makes the Link Marked Volume. It decides that one reference's Volume and doesn't extend Record ranges.

## Open questions (decide before agent work)

- Where the control goes: in the toolbar beside the Volume name ("This Volume"), or in the Volume switcher.
- What a later Image Mark on the same reference does to the Volume-only pin. It probably replaces it, since an Image says more.
- Whether a Volume-only Mark is offered for references with a page too, where an Image Mark is usually possible.
- Undoing it: back to the Volume by years or Record range.
