# A Conversion makes one Entry

- `app-citations.convert` makes one Entry for every citation of the record, with the Mark's Box and the Transcription, and points each Citation at it; their original pages stay.
- Record pins (`myheritage/pins/marks.ts`) read converted Citations as now.
