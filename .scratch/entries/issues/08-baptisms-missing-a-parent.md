# List the baptisms missing a parent

A check list in the Church books tab: each baptism Entry whose child has a father or mother in the tree who isn't named in it. Opening one goes to its Box, where _Edit_ adds them. One left out on purpose (the book doesn't name the father) can be said so in the Transcription or a Reminder.
