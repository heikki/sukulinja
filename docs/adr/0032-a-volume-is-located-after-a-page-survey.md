# A Volume is located after a page survey

With nothing shipped (ADR-0031), an unpinned Volume's estimates are counted from its first Image and often land several Images off, beyond the three either way Vision Match looks (ADR-0029). Heikki (2026-11-15) wanted page numbers read by the vision model, and the references worked through a book at a time: download the Volume, then locate its references, the page numbering read first.

- **_Locate estimates in this Volume_** is in the Scan viewer's menu beside _Locate estimates on this Image_, while the Assistant is on. It first runs a **Page survey** of the Volume, reloads the Volume's citations so its estimates count from what the survey read, then locates each Image's estimates in turn as _on this Image_ does, one question for the Image and its neighbours while some aren't found. One summary tells how many it found in the Volume and on how many Images the survey read Page numbers, and lists those it didn't find with why, by the Image each is estimated on, each opening its reference (2026-11-16); _Undo all_ takes back every Match, as before. A survey is not run at download or import: only when asked, so a model is read only for a Volume someone works through.
- **A Page survey** reads the Page numbers on Images spread over the Volume, from 5% into it to 95% (past covers and front matter), at most 20 Images apart; then, between any two readings that don't agree exactly with the book's pace (one number an Image for a spread, two for pages), the Image halfway, until every two neighbouring readings agree or have no Image left between them, or it has made 50 readings. Exactly (amended 2026-11-16): a slack of two pages left estimates an Image off where a leaf was missing or inserted, and Heikki's first try missed an entry so; one leaf costs a few readings to close in on. Where the numbering starts over (a Page run, ADR-0031) it closes in on the place. An unreadable Image is skipped for one beside it. It always surveys the whole Volume (Heikki, 2026-11-16), but closes in first where a page the dataset's estimates there cite could be, so a survey cut short by its readings is short where none is cited; and it reads four Images at a time. A second numbering on the Images (amended 2026-11-16: a book on printed forms, its form numbers in one corner and its page numbers by hand in the other, read both or either) is told apart by its line: of each Image's numbers, the survey takes those whose pace and offset most of the readings near it share; a reading agreeing with neither neighbour, where they agree across it, is a misreading and left out. The reader is also told to give only the handwritten numbers there.
- **What it reads is saved as Page pins** by the vision model, in the data folder's pins, for every dataset; a hand pin wins over one, as for any vision pin. Readings are cached with the others (`.vision-readings`), so a survey run again asks only what it hasn't read.
- **_Survey page numbers_** (2026-11-16, Heikki, to try the survey on its own) runs only the survey of the Volume shown, from the same menu, and tells on how many Images it read Page numbers and whether they all agree. It reads every Image anew each time, kept readings aside, and its Page pins replace the Volume's earlier vision ones (2026-11-16, Heikki); hand ones stay.
- The survey lives with the Reader, in `myheritage/vision/survey.ts`, and the data folder's scans save it (`surveyPages`): a download stays Astia's alone.

## Considered options

- **Survey at import.** Rejected: an import doesn't reach Astia, works without a key, and would read Volumes no one opens.
- **Survey on its own as each download finishes.** Rejected for now (Heikki chose asking): it reads a model without being asked.
- **Read the page number on each Image Vision Match asks about instead.** Leaves a Volume's numbering unknown until each reference is searched, and costs a reading per search rather than a few per Volume.
