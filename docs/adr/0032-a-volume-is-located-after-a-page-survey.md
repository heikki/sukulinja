# A Volume is located after a page survey

With nothing shipped (ADR-0031), an unpinned Volume's estimates are counted from its first Image and often land several Images off, beyond the three either way Vision Match looks (ADR-0029). Heikki (2026-11-15) wanted page numbers read by the vision model, and the references worked through a book at a time: download the Volume, then locate its references, the page numbering read first.

- **_Locate estimates in this Volume_** is in the Scan viewer's menu beside _Locate estimates on this Image_, while the Assistant is on. It first runs a **Page survey** of the Volume where none of its Images has its pages read or set yet (amended 2026-11-16, Heikki: where some have, it counts from them, and a survey is asked for on its own), reloads the Volume's citations so its estimates count from what the survey read, then locates each Image's estimates in turn as _on this Image_ does, one question for the Image and its neighbours while some aren't found (amended 2026-11-17: the server runs these steps as one operation, telling the Scan viewer each as it goes, so the survey moving the estimates and the search where they then are is one place, tested as one). One summary tells how many it found in the Volume and on how many Images the survey read Page numbers, and lists those it didn't find with why, by the Image each is estimated on, each opening its reference (2026-11-16); _Undo all_ takes back every Match, as before. A survey is not run at download or import: only when asked, so a model is read only for a Volume someone works through.
- **A Page survey** reads the Page numbers on Images spread over the Volume, from 5% into it to 95% (past covers and front matter), at most 20 Images apart; then, between any two readings that don't agree exactly with the book's pace (one number an Image for a spread, two for pages), the Image halfway, until every two neighbouring readings agree or have no Image left between them, or it has made 50 readings. Exactly (amended 2026-11-16): a slack of two pages left estimates an Image off where a leaf was missing or inserted, and Heikki's first try missed an entry so; one leaf costs a few readings to close in on. Where the numbering starts over (a Page run, ADR-0031) it closes in on the place. An unreadable Image is skipped for one beside it. It always surveys the whole Volume (Heikki, 2026-11-16), but closes in first where a page the dataset's estimates there cite could be, so a survey cut short by its readings is short where none is cited; and it reads four Images at a time. A second numbering on the Images (amended 2026-11-16: a book on printed forms, its form numbers in one corner and its page numbers by hand in the other, read both or either) is told apart by its line: of each Image's numbers, the survey takes those whose pace and offset most of the readings near it share; a reading agreeing with neither neighbour, where they agree across it, is a misreading and left out. The reader gives each number it sees with whether it's handwritten, and the handwritten ones are taken where there are any: told only to leave the printed ones out, Haiku still read them on most Images of such a book. Asked for each number's kind, it still mostly gave the printed ones, the handwritten too small in the scaled spread: so the reader is sent only the spread's top left corner, sharp (the top quarter of its height, 40% of its width), with a short question (Heikki, 2026-11-16: a spread numbered once has its number there, and from the left page's number the whole book follows); asked of both top corners, even each on its own, the model still took the bold printed number. The survey tells a book numbering pages from one numbering spreads by how the readings step, 2 an Image or 1, and gives each Image of a page-numbered book its right page too. Tested image by image against 61 known page numbers in two books on printed forms (2026-11-16), that corner still held the form's entry numbers and column headings, and Haiku often answered with them: so the corner is tighter (12% of the height, 20% of the width), enlarged to what the models take, and the model reads the number digit by digit. That read 28–29 of 37 and 18–19 of 24 exactly, from 13 and 17; the handwritten flag went, the corner holding no printed number to tell apart. Most misses are one digit off and fall off the line; none is pinned that no neighbour agrees with. A four-digit number read without its leading digits, as 127 where the readings near it give 1127, has them put back: such misses came in runs that agreed with each other and were taken for the numbering starting over. So is any digit but the last read wrong, as 915 for 215, or read twice, as 2446 for 246 (eight more books tested, 2026-11-16; an ornate hand misread many), but only towards a line at least three of the readings near it share, more than share the reading's own: a lone pair misread alike once pulled right readings to it. The last digit read wrong is left as it is, as a leaf missing or inserted reads the same. Where the corner holds no number, the top of the left page is asked, as some books write "Pagina 12" in its middle.
- **What it reads is saved as Page pins** by the vision model, in the data folder's pins, for every dataset; a hand pin wins over one, as for any vision pin. Readings are cached with the others (`.vision-readings`), so a survey run again asks only what it hasn't read.
- **_Survey page numbers_** (2026-11-16, Heikki, to try the survey on its own) runs only the survey of the Volume shown, from the same menu, and tells on how many Images it read Page numbers and whether they all agree. It reads every Image anew each time, kept readings aside, and its Page pins replace the Volume's earlier vision ones (2026-11-16, Heikki), and hand ones too (amended 2026-11-16, Heikki: a new survey starts the numbering over), after the Scan viewer has said how many pages set by hand it takes away and been told to go on.
- The survey lives with the Reader, in `myheritage/vision/survey.ts`, and the data folder's Volume facts save it (`citations/volume-facts/`, amended 2026-11-24): a download stays Astia's alone.

## Considered options

- **Survey at import.** Rejected: an import doesn't reach Astia, works without a key, and would read Volumes no one opens.
- **Survey on its own as each download finishes.** Rejected for now (Heikki chose asking): it reads a model without being asked.
- **Read the page number on each Image Vision Match asks about instead.** Leaves a Volume's numbering unknown until each reference is searched, and costs a reading per search rather than a few per Volume.

Amended 2026-11-16: the Scan viewer shows, beside the Image number, the pages the Volume's Page pins put on the Image shown, as the estimates count them: plain where a pin is on that Image (its tooltip says read by the vision model or pinned by hand), "≈" where counted from the pins around it. Stepping through a Volume then shows where a survey's readings or the counting between them are off (Heikki asked for it to check surveys).

Amended 2026-11-16 (Heikki): the page is a see-through bubble over the scan's top left corner, behind a Page numbers switch in Settings, not behind the Assistant: without pins it shows the book's own numbering, as the estimates count it. Clicked, it takes the page written there, or a spread's two ("4-5"), and saves it as a hand Page pin, which wins over the vision model's and which a survey keeps; left empty, it sets the Image to have no page number (amended 2026-11-16, Heikki: a cover, a blank or inserted leaf), and the Volume's pages are counted as if it weren't filmed; a page set so replaces only a pin on that Image, not the same page on another nearby (amended 2026-11-16, Heikki: a book may have a page twice, filmed twice or numbered so); a × beside a hand one takes it away, and (amended 2026-11-16, Heikki) beside one the vision model read, the Image then counted from the pins around it; a later survey may read it again. So a user without the vision model can place a Volume's estimates by hand, one Image at a time, the Images around counted from it.

Amended 2026-11-16 (Heikki): an Image set to have no page reads "skipped" in the bubble, which serves a page filmed twice too. Where the bubble is shown, the Scan viewer's menu offers _Clear page numbers_ while any Image of the Volume has its pages read or set: it says on how many, and once told to go on, takes every Page pin of the Volume's filming away, hand and the vision model's, so its pages are counted as its book is numbered again.
